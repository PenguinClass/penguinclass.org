**Event:** TAYC Frostbite Regatta  
**Date:** November 6, 2021  
**Venue:** Tred Avon Yacht Club

Results for the 2021 TAYC Frostbite regatta are available in the [Class Archive](/archive/legacy-website/2021%5FTAYC%5FPenguin%20and%20Comet%20Frostbite.htm).
