# Archive

Welcome to the Penguin Class Archive. This section contains historical content and legacy materials organized by category.

## Archive Contents

### [Legacy Website](/archive/legacy-website/)
The complete legacy website from penguinclass.com, including all historical pages, results, and documents.

### [Results](/results/)
Historical race results and championship records from 2007 to present.

### [Gallery](/gallery/)
Historical photos and event galleries organized by year and event.

### [Minutes](/docs/minutes/)
Class meeting minutes and official records.

### [Documents](/docs/)
Official class documents, forms, and reference materials.

### [Yearbooks](/class/yearbooks/)
Historical yearbooks and annual publications.

## Archive Organization

The archive is organized to provide easy access to historical content while maintaining clear separation between current and legacy materials. The legacy website content is preserved in its original form for historical reference, while current content is integrated into the main site structure.

For questions about archive content or to contribute historical materials, please contact the [Class Secretary](/class/officers/).
