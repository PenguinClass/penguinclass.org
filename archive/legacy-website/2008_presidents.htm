Presidents/Leukemia Cup Results

Paul,
 
It would have been great to have more boats and more wind, but we had a good time for a good cause with three boats and 0-6 with the occasional gust of 10.  Three boats came and four races were competitively sailed.  Every sailor won a race and struggled to read the drastically shifty breeze that read from all directions on the compass throughout the day.  Most of the time it came down to who reacted to the shift or got to the puff first and sometimes it seemed like dumb luck.  Let's have more participation for this one next year

Scott
