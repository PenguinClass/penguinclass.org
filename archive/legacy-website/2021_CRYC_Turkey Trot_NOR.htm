NOTICE OF RACE

 

CORSICA RIVER YACHT CLUB

Turkey Trot Regatta

Saturday 20 November, 2021

 

Corsica River Sailing Center at Ship Point

589 Conquest Road, Centreville, MD 21617

 

 

Classes:           Comet and Penguin class boats

Rules:              The regatta will be governed by the rules as defined in the Racing Rules of                                    Sailing (RRS) and the respective class rules.

Awards:          Trophies for skippers/crew for places 1-3

Entry Fee:       $25

Registration:  Register at Regatta Network

Races may be cancelled for a class if less than three (3) boats have registered for class by Thursday, November18

Schedule:        Five races are scheduled with a throw out if 5 races completed,

10:00 AM        Skippers meeting

11:00 AM        First warning

Social:             Complementary chili and cornbread and drinks after racing

                                   

Awards:          Trophy presentation during social

Contact:          Art Silcox, (571)213-9536  art.silcox@gmail.com

DIRECTIONS:

From western shore:  Route 50 East over Bay Bridge. Stay to left on Rt. 301 at Rout 50/301 split.

Go to Rt. 213 North through Centreville to Spaniard Neck Road (across from North Brooke entrance). Turn left and go approximately 3 miles to right turn in road, proceed approx. 0.5 miles and turn left.  (you will see Conquest Beach sign) Continue on this road approx. 1 mile to Corsica River Sailing Center on left.
