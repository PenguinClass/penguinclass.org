Oct 18, 2009

Hello Everyone

 

Day three turned out to a perfect day for Penguin sailing. Just four boats out for a Farewell to Tenner sail.

As the racing started Bill pointed out to us that a bald eagle was flying around over the lagoon.

 

We sailed seven races in West to Southwest wind between 7 and 13 knots, when it was touching the water.

The fans were on and off all afternoon. There was no telling where the new wind was going to be.

Great racing was enjoyed by all.

 

Hope to see you on the water next Sunday.
