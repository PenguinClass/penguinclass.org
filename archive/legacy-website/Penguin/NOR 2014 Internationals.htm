NOTICE OF RACE

International Penguin Class Dinghy Association

2014 International Championship

                                             August 16 - 17, 2014

 

                             Hosted by:   Corsica River Yacht Club

 

Rules:

This Regatta is sanctioned by the International Penguin Class Dinghy Association (IPCDA), and will be conducted by the Corsica River Yacht Club.  The Regatta shall be governed by The Racing Rules of Sailing (RRS), including the US Sailing prescriptions, and the IPCDA Class rules except as modified by this Notice of Race and the Sailing Instructions.

 

The following 5 items shall apply:


l. Minimum crew weight is 290 lbs for classic boats and 310 lbs. for modern boats*. Minimum hull weight is 140 lbs. Excess hull weight may be applied towards the crew weight

2. Any boat that capsizes during a race, as defined by the mast hitting the water, shall retire from that race.
3. Anchors are not required.
4. One serviceable paddle is required.

5. A painter NLT 25’ and 3/16” diameter is required.

 

Notice is hereby given that all participants, including spectators, officials and others participate in this event including pre- and post-race activities entirely at their own risk.

 

Advertising and Sponsors:

The Regatta is designated Category A in accordance with Rule 79 of the RRS.  There will not be a penalty for items that are approved by the Organizing Authority.

 

 

Eligibility:

International Championship:  The skipper shall be a member in good standing of the IPCDA and have acknowledgement of payment of 2014 dues. For skippers sailing with non-family member minor crew (under the age of 19), the name and emergency phone numbers of the parents will be required or you can complete a Parental Permission Form. Juniors (19 or under) may also sail in the “Senior” International Championship, however to be eligible for senior awards, they must meet the senior weight rule.

 

Entry:

           

International Championship                                                                                 $50.00 US                         

                                                                       

 

Sailing Instructions:

Sailing Instructions will be available at registration at 9:00 AM on Saturday August 16th, 2014.

 

Measurements:

Measurements will be conducted from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM  on Saturday August 16th, 2014. Measurement will include but are not limited to hull weight, crew weight, and safety gear.

 

Scoring:

The low point scoring system with no bonus points from the RRS Appendix A will be used.  In the event that six or more races are sailed, there will be a provision for a throwout.

 

Prizes:

Awards will be presented to the top five finishers overall. Several other special awards will be presented and include the Grand Master’s Award (highest finisher over the age of 60), the top Women skipper, top parent-child team, and top classic boat.
