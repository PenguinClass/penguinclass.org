Penguin Sailors:

 

 

The first Maple Hall Regatta is scheduled for Saturday, May 17th with a 10:30 skippers meeting. Racing will follow until 2:30 or 3:00. Then the proprietor of Maple Hall, Rick Rhine has invited us to a cookout. He will provide Hamburgers, Hot Dogs and salad. If you want soft drinks or beer please BYO. Monty Baker has generously donated awards for this regatta and Dick Kelly will run the races.

 

Maple Hall is located on Tilghman Creek www.maplehall.com  By land go to St. Michaels and continue on 33. After about 3 miles when the road bears left continue straight to Claiborne. Maple Hall is on the right just as you arrive at Claiborne. Go down the lane and keep left around the house to the water.

 

Please let me know if you plan to come so I can give an approximate head count to Rick. Bring your family, friends and prospective Penguin sailors.

 

Thanks and see you on Saturday.

 

Paul
