Registration

Advance registration is suggested and preferred for all fleets.

All Advance Registered sailors must check in Saturday morning 8:30-9:30 to pick up meal tickets.

Mail Registration: Please complete entry form and mail to Christopher Ford, 6926 Montgomery Road,

Elkridge, MD 21075. Early registration must be postmarked by Saturday, August 18, 2007. Include

your check for entry fee and any additional meal tickets. Make checks payable to WRSC. Lower fee with

early registration, but no refunds are possible.

Online Registration: Go to https://www.regattanetwork.com/clubmgmt/applet_club_events.php?

CLUB_ID=97

Or www.wrsc.org Online Registration closes on Saturday, August 18th at 11:59 p.m. No refunds are possible.

In Person Registration: Participants may also register at WRSC on Friday 8/31/07, 8:00 AM- 9:00 AM

(for Annapolis race to Galesville only), or Friday, 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM or Sat 9/1/07, 8:30AM – 9:30 AM

(for weekend races). Regatta sailing instructions will be available during registration on Friday and Saturday

mornings at WRSC.
