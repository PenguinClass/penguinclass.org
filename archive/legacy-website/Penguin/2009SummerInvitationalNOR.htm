Host Yacht Club:                 Tred Avon Yacht Club, Oxford, MD

Classes:                                                Club 420, Laser, Laser Radial, Optimist, Penguin

 

Awards:                                                Awards are provided for the top three finishes of each class/fleet with at least five registered boats.

 

Entry Deadlines:                                  Registration closes at 0930 on day of regatta.

CBYRA Members                Fee         $15.00 (singles) and $25.00 (doubles)

Non-CBYRA Members       Fee         $20.00 (singles) and $30.00 (doubles)

                                                Fees include a continental breakfast. 

 

Schedule of Events:

Registration                         0830-0930            Location                clubhouse continental breakfast

Skipper’s Meeting              0930                       Location                clubhouse

First Warning                       The first warning will immediately follow the Skippers’ Meeting.

No start after                       1430

Social Activity                     

Awards Ceremony             Awards will take place as soon as practical at the club.            

 

Lunch:                                                   Lunch will be available for purchase.

 

Boat Drop-off & Launching:             Drop-off & launch at two beaches and two hoists.

 

Trailer Storage:                                   Store trailers on the west beach or outside the main gate.

 

Directions:                                            From Rt. 50 to Easton (north or south), take Rt. 322 (Easton Bypass) to

Oxford Rd (Rt 333). Turn right & follow signs to Oxford. Drive through town to waterfront. Turn left & club is on the right. OBEY the 25 mph speed limit in town!

 

Accommodations:

Hotels                                    Several hotels in Easton, MD

Housing                                 Not Available

Camping                               Not Available

 

Dining:                                                   Club facilities are available for participating sailors and families.

 

 

Special Notes:                                     A parent host will be available to answer questions, provide directions to

local businesses, etc. We will do everything we can to help you get your junior sailor(s) on the water  - happy and ready to race.
