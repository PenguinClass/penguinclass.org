Hello Everyone

 

Day 4 Sunday the 23rd was an exciting day at the Lagoon seven boats sail seven races in West winds around 5 to 12 knots,

with some stronger gusts. Tom and Ed handled the RC boat and set up the course.

It was great to see all of you out sailing.

 

Day 5 Sunday the 30th was one of those days when you know you shouldn't go out sailing, but four of us left our better judgment at the dock and gave it a go anyways. Wind was Southwest at 10 to 15 with gusts around 20 knots. This was a challenging day and with the weather getting worse we decided to stop after the fourth race.

 

This Sunday will be the Cold N' Nasty Regatta. We will start at 1:00 PM. Daylight saving time changes so don't forget to change the clocks.

Please try to be there and launch early so we can start on time.

Before we leave the dock I would like for us all to be sure to weight up the boats evenly.

 

Hope to see you this Sunday

10/09/2011

Hello Everyone

 

The Second Annual Tennerstedt Regatta sailed on Sunday Oct 9.

Seven boats sailed seven races under sunny skies in 5 to 10 knot winds from the East. Thanks to Ed for setting up the course and for starting and scoring the races.  All boats were aggressive at the starting line with someone over early in almost every race. We had a few general recalls where almost everyone was over early. When the wind speed changes in the stating sequence it can be challenging to get the timing just right. We all enjoyed the close racing and the good competition.

After the races we took the opportunity to spend some time with the Tennerstedt family and share some of our favorite Tenner stories which I am sure you all enjoyed as much as I did. Only after hearing the group share these great memories for a while was I able to understand how much he was involved with this class and what great fun it was to race against him. We are all thankful for everything he did for this fleet and for the class. Thank you to Mark Kastel for the suggestion of having a Tenner regatta, I think it was a great idea.

 

Hope to see you all next Sunday

10/02/2011

 

Hello Everyone

 

The series opened with a perfect day for Penguin sailing at the lagoon.

Wind was on and off, from the East about 5 knots when it was on. Six boats made it to the starting line.

Six races were sailed out of which Jack was able to toss a 2nd place

to leave the day with 5 points. That was nice of you to show us how it is done.

 

Thank you to everyone that sailed and to everyone that helped with launching and hauling the boats.

 

We are looking forward to another great sailing day next Sunday.

October 9th will be The 2nd Annual Tennerstedt Regatta.

 

Hope to see you there.
