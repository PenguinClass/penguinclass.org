Miles River Yacht Club

Annual 2008

August 2nd.

Early morning thunderstorms put a damper on attendance, but the clouds cleared and the few who showed up were treated to four very close and competitive races at the Miles River Yacht Club Annual regatta.  Bud Dailey won, but was closely followed by Bill Lane with Ed Lutz only a point behind.  Reports are that the racing was close, with the three boats finishing either overlapped or within a few boat lengths
