Corsica River One Design Regatta

October 10 and 11 2025

 

CORSICA RIVER YACHT CLUB

Centerville MD

NOTICE OF RACE

 

1    RULES

1.1 The regatta will be governed by the rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing 2025-2028 and the respective class association rules. 

1.2 The sailing instructions will consist of the instructions in RRS Appendix S, Standard Sailing instructions, and any supplementary sailing instructions that will be posted on the official notice board located at Corsica River Yacht Club. 

1.3     This regatta is designated Category A in accordance with RRS Appendix J1.2(2), ISAF Regulation 20.

2   ADVERTISING

Competitor advertising will not be restricted.

3 ELIGIBILITY AND ENTRY

       Eligibility: There will be starts for each one-design class if 5 or more boats are registered, if less than 3 boats are registered for a class/division by Oct 1, we reserve the right to move those to a Portsmith class or refund entry.

6       MEASUREMENT

Each boat shall comply with their class requirements 

7       SAILING INSTRUCTIONS

The sailing instructions will be available after Oct 1 on regatta network or at CRYC club on Saturday

8   THE COURSES

The courses to be sailed will be drop mark Windward/Leeward or Triangular courses as described in the Sailing Instructions.  The races will be on Chester or Corsica Rivers.

9       PENALTY SYSTEM

9.1        The Scoring Penalty, RRS 44.3, will apply.

9.2        Decisions of the protest committee will be final as provided in RRS 70.5.

10    SCORING The Low Point Scoring System of Appendix A2 of the RRS will apply except as modified by this: if less than 6 races no score will be excluded.

19 PRIZES

Awards: Trophies will be awarded for all classes 

Trophy places per class will be determined based on numbers registered for each class one week prior to the event. The organizing authority will review the registered competitors in each class and decide on the final trophy order and count. General guidelines are as follows: 3 boats, one place awarded. 4 boats, two places awarded. 5+ boats, three places awarded.
