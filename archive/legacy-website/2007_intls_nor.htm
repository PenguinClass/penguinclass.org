NOTICE OF RACE

International Penguin Class Dinghy Association

2007 International Championship

August 24 – 26, 2007

Hosted by: Tred Avon Yacht Club

Oxford, Maryland

Rules:
This Regatta is sanctioned by the International Penguin Class Dinghy Association (IPCDA), and will be conducted by IPCDA and hosted by the Tred Avon Yacht Club . The Regatta shall be governed by The Racing Rules of Sailing 2005-2008 (RRS), and the IPCDA Class rules except as modified by this Notice of Race and the Sailing Instructions.

The following 4 items shall apply:
l. Minimum crew weight is 290 lbs for classic boats and 310 lbs. for modern boats*. Minimum hull weight is 140 lbs. Excess hull weight may be applied towards the crew weight

2. Any boat that capsizes during a race, as defined by the mast hitting the water, shall retire from that race.
3. Anchors are not required.
4. One serviceable paddle is required
Notice is hereby given that all participants, including spectators, officials and others, participate in this event including pre- and post-race activities entirely at their own risk.

*Modern boat definition includes Burtis, Innovator, & Freedom hulls. Class measurer or his designate shall make classification decisions. * Higher weight limit for modern boat follows guidelines adopted for Region III.

Advertising and Sponsors:
The Regatta is designated Category A in accordance with Rule 79 of the RRS. There will not be a penalty for items that are approved by the Organizing Authority.

Eligibility:
International Championship: The skipper shall be a member in good standing of the IPCDA with proof of payment of 2006 dues. For skippers sailing with non-family member minor crew (under the age of 19), the name and emergency phone numbers of the parents will be required or you can complete a Parental Permission Form. Juniors may also sail in the “Senior” International Championship, however to be eligible for senior awards, they must meet the senior weight rule.

Sailing Instructions:
Sailing Instructions will be available at registration on Friday, August 24, 2007.
Measurements:
Measurements will be conducted from 8:30 AM to 10:00 AM on Friday, August 24, 2007. Measurements may include but are not limited to hull weight, crew weight, sails, and safety gear. All equipment and measurement items are subject to measurement at any point up to the awards presentation.

Scoring:
The low point scoring system with no bonus points from the RRS Appendix A will be used. In the event that six or more races are sailed, there will be a provision for a throw-out. Three races shall constitute a series for the International Championships.

Prizes:
Awards will be presented to the top five finishers overall and to the top junior team in the Senior Championships. Awards will also be given in the President’s division: skippers who have not placed third or better in a regional or higher event. Several other special awards will be presented and include the Master’s Award (highest skipper born prior to August 24th, 1957), the top woman skipper, top parent-child team and the Rookie award to the best finisher who has never sailed in an IPCDA International championship.
