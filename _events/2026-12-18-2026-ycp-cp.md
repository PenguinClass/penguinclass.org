The 2026 Campeonato Paulista (São Paulo State Championship) of the Penguin Class — Classe Pinguim — will be sailed December 18–20, 2026 at Yacht Club Paulista (founded 1932) on the Represa de Guarapiranga in São Paulo, Brazil.

The Brazilian Penguin fleet for this race includes four new fiberglass hulls and one classic wood hull (#9214), with the new boats to be measured in November.

**Offer to American sailors:** The organizers would like to offer one of the new boats to an American representative, and Yacht Club Paulista has two beds available for competitors. Interested sailors should respond **by November 1, 2026** to Andre Pinheiro at [andrericardopinheiro@gmail.com](mailto:andrericardopinheiro@gmail.com).
