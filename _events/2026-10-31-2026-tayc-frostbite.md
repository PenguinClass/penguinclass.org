The 2026 TAYC Frostbite Regatta will be held at Tred Avon Yacht Club, with Penguins and Comets racing on Saturday, October 31, 2026 (the 2.4mR and Sonar classes race October 29–31).

- Saturday schedule: continental breakfast, registration closes, skippers' meeting, first warning 1100, awards after racing
- Entry fee: $55 for Penguins and Comets
- Awards with light hors d'oeuvres and cash bar at the TAYC clubhouse after racing

Registration is on ClubSpot via the event page.
