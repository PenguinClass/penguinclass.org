The 2025 Penguin Internationals will be held at Corsica River Yacht Club on September 13-14, 2025.

This is the premier event of the Penguin Class, bringing together sailors from across the country and around the world to compete for the International Championship title.

## Event Details
- **Dates:** September 13-14, 2025
- **Venue:** Corsica River Yacht Club
- **Location:** Centreville, Maryland, U.S.A.
- **Registration:** [Regatta Network](https://www.regattanetwork.com/event/30156)

## Registration
Register online through Regatta Network. Early registration is encouraged.

## Notice of Race
The Notice of Race is available in the [Class Archive](/archive/legacy-website/2025_Intls_NOR.htm).

## Accommodations
Local accommodations and camping options will be available. Contact the organizing authority for recommendations.
