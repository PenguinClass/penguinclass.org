The 2025 Corsica Annual Regatta will be held at Corsica River Yacht Club on October 11, 2025.

This annual event is a favorite among local and regional Penguin sailors, offering competitive racing in the beautiful waters of the Corsica River.

## Event Details
- **Date:** October 11, 2025
- **Venue:** Corsica River Yacht Club
- **Location:** Centreville, Maryland, U.S.A.

## Registration
Registration details will be available closer to the event date.

## Contact
For questions about the event, please contact the organizing authority or visit the [Corsica River Yacht Club website](https://corsicariveryachtclub.org/).
