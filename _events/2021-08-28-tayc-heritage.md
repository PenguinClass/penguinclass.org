The 2021 TAYC Heritage Regatta was held at Tred Avon Yacht Club on August 28, 2021.

This event also served as the Region III Championship.

## Event Details
- **Date:** August 28, 2021
- **Venue:** Tred Avon Yacht Club
- **Location:** Oxford, Maryland, U.S.A.
- **Championship:** Region III Championship

## Results
Results for the 2021 TAYC Heritage Regatta are available in the [Class Archive](/archive/legacy-website/2021%5FHeritage.htm).

## Event Summary
The TAYC Heritage Regatta celebrates the rich history of sailing at Tred Avon Yacht Club, bringing together sailors for competitive racing and fellowship. This year's event also determined the Region III Champion.
