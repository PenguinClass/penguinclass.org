Welcome to the 2026 Admiral Byrd Regatta!

We are excited to host this year's regatta and welcome all racers.  This year's regatta will be held during the weekend of July 18 and 19.  

The regatta is open to following one design classes:
- A Class Catamarans
- Albacore
- Buccaneer
- Chesapeake 20
- C 420
- Flying Scot
- Hamptons
- Laser Ilca 7
- Optis (Green Fleet - Novice )  A separate course will be set up for the Junior Green fleet right by the club house.  Parents will easily be able to watch all the action. 
- Penguins
- Shields

[Note:  We are open to expanding the number of classes. If you would like for us to consider a class, please send an email to sailing@cambridgeyachtclub.com. Depending on the level of interest and our ability to support an additional class, we will make a decision by June 1st. An email will be sent out accordingly on any decision made. ]  

We will attempt as many races as possible each day.  The races will be held just outside of the yacht club so families can view the action right from our docks.
