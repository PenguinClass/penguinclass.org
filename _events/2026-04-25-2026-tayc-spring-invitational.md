Welcome to the Tred Avon Yacht Club in Oxford, MD for the 2026 Spring Invitational Regatta. We are situated on the beautiful Eastern Shore of Maryland, racing on the Tred Avon and Choptank Rivers.  We welcome the Comet (Sat only), Penguin (Sat only), Sonar (Fri-Sat) and 2.4mR (Fri, Sat, Sun) classes to race over the 24-26 April weekend.
