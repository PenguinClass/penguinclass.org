The 2021 Corsica River Frostbite / Atlantic Coast Championship was held at Corsica River Yacht Club on November 20, 2021.

This event also served as the Atlantic Coast Championship.

## Event Details
- **Date:** November 20, 2021
- **Venue:** Corsica River Yacht Club
- **Location:** Centreville, Maryland, U.S.A.
- **Championship:** Atlantic Coast Championship

Results for the 2021 Corsica River Frostbite / Atlantic Coast Championship are available in the [Class Archive](/archive/legacy-website/2021%20Corsica%20River%20Yacht%20Club%20Turkey%20Trot%20Regatta.htm).

## Event Summary
The Corsica River Frostbite regatta combines competitive racing with the challenge of late-season conditions, and this year also determined the Atlantic Coast Champion.
