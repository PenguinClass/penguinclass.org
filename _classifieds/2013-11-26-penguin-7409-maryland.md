## Penguin 7409 - Roberts Industries - Olney, MD

**Location:** Olney, Maryland  
**Price:** $600  

Penguin class sailboat. Length 11'6", beam 5". Cat rigged dinghy-type centerboard sailboat. Fiberglass hull, Aluminum mast, wooden boom, all rigging intact. Sailable as is. New paint and new floor board would be good. Trailer has some rust but is in good condition with new wheels, tires and bearings. Great day sailer.

The manufacturer was Roberts Industries of Stratford, Connecticut. The sail (hull?) number is 7409.
