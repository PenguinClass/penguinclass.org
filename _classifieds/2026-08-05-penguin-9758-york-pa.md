# Penguin 9758

**Location:** York, Pennsylvania  
**Price:** Best offer

Beautiful wood Penguin #9758, built in 2010 from cypress, mahogany, and marine-grade plywood. The boat has been sailed only a couple of times and is in like-new condition.

Features include an aluminum mast and boom, Amsteel standing rigging, and two sails: one supplied by the Penguin Class and another made by Somerset Sails in New York. There is no trailer. The seller may be able to deliver as far as Maryland if the boat can be loaded in a pickup truck.

Questions and requests for additional photos are welcome. Watch the [two-minute build video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZxjzD7wUBzE).
