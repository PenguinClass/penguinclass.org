## Event Details

September 13 – 14, 2026

The Corsica River Yacht Club in Centreville, Maryland, hosted the 2025
Internationals. Regatta chair John Foster and many CRYC members rolled
out the red carpet for the 6 (plus 1) teams that registered and
competed. John registered his restored Penguin \#666, aka “The Beast”,
although the boat stayed ashore while John tended to weather mark
duties. The weather mark boat was kept busy setting or changing the mark
as PRO Roy Sherman strived to keep the course square in the unstable
westerly breeze. Jonathan and Annie Bartlett got off to a strong start,
winning the first two races, and then came back from an OCS in race 3,
forced the wrong way, which turned out to be the right way to win that
race as well. Guessing became somewhat the order of the day for the
final two races with the breeze fading, trending south, fading, and
otherwise just being unpredictable. Charlie Krafft, sailing with Finn
Besack, had a string of seconds, and a lead in race 4, that turned into
playing catch up with the fleet as the wind filled in from the opposite
side of the course. In the early races, Bill Lawson, sailing with his
wife Colette Preis, traded 3rd & 4th place with Bob Tan sailing with his
wife Nora Beck Tan. Collin Kirby sailing with Celeste Palumbo had a
string of fifths, sailing Chris Conway’s boat, as the Class-owned boat
Collin has been working on was not ready in time for the event. Scott
Leppert sailing with his son Elliott rounded out the small but
competitive fleet, with a pair of 3<sup>rd’s</sup> despite carrying 100
extra pounds of crew weight. In race 4, the Lawson team took advantage
of a southerly shift to take the win, followed by the Bartletts’ who
just managed to get ahead of the Lepperts’ on the final run of the twice
around windward leeward course.

Saturday evening, the contestants were treated to Eastern shore
hospitality with a BBQ dinner with fixin’s.

Sunday dawned bright and clear, but mostly windless. With zephyrs
appearing and lots of optimism on the part of the RC, the fleet
eventually headed out when the breeze started to fill in, only to find
that with unstable direction and velocity it wasn’t going to be possible
to set a course so the N over A flags were hoisted and the fleet paddled
in. As fate would have it, the breeze returned shortly after the fleet
had derigged but the initial flush of wind faded and the committee’s
decision was reaffirmed. With leftovers for lunch, the trophies were
awarded. A big thanks to CRYC and PRO Roy Sherman and regatta chair John
Foster. Also, thanks to Jono Roberts for the novel trophies:
laser-etched repurposed and refinished cigar boxes from Jono’s trophy
enterprise. Congratulations to Jonathan and Annie on yet another
Internationals win.
